Overview

This webpage provides supplementary materials for Section 4 (Experiments) of the paper 

H. Nabeshima, M. Banbara, T. Schaub, and T. Soh: The Magnificent ASP-based Nurse Scheduling System at the University of Yamanashi Hospital, submitted to ICLP 2025.

Search Strategies

In addition to the three strategies presented in the paper (LNPS, MP, MP-IS), we introduce two additional strategies, MP-PS and PS, making a total of five search strategies for comparison.

  • LNPS (Large Neighborhood Prioritized Search) [Sugimori et al. 2024]
    • In our LNPS implementation, if no improved solution is found within a given time interval \(t\) (\(t \in \{10,30,60\}\)), the search is automatically restarted. Each configuration is denoted as LNPS-\(t\) based on the selected time threshold.
  • MP (Minimal Perturbation)
    • This search strategy minimizes modifications to the initial solution by introducing a penalty term into the objective function.
    • In this experiment, we set three priority levels for the objective function: highest, intermediate, and lowest, denoted as MP-High, MP-Mid, and MP-Low, respectively.
  • MP-IS (MP with Initial-value-based Search)
    • This search strategy extends MP by incorporating an initial-value-based search, where retained assignments from the initial solution guide the search.
    • In the early stage of the search, to reproduce the assignments from the initial solution, we assign a larger-than-default value to the initial state of the variable selection heuristic VSIDS. We define three variations of this initial value: 1, 10, and 100, denoted as IS1, IS10, and IS100, respectively.
    • Furthermore, similar to MP, we define three variations based on priority: MP-ISXX-High, MP-ISXX-Mid, and MP-ISXX-Low.
  • MP-PS (MP with Prioritized Search)
    • This search strategy extends MP by incorporating prioritized search. Similar to LNPS, it prioritizes the reproduction of retained assignments. As with MP, we define three variations: MP-PS-High, MP-PS-Mid, and MP-PS-Low.
    • While MP-IS reproduces the assignments from the initial solution only in the early stage of the search, MP-PS maintains this throughout all search phases.
  • PS (Prioritized Search)
    • This search strategy is a variant of LNPS without automatic restarts.
    • It is included to demonstrate the impact of automatic restarts.
